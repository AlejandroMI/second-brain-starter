# Tiny Community Experiments

## Idea

Test small, time-boxed gatherings that help neighbors learn together without first creating a formal organization.

## Why It Matters

Starting with one useful event makes it possible to learn what people want before building infrastructure.

## Possible Uses

- A four-person public-domain reading club.
- A monthly repair or skill-share hour.
- A neighborhood walk with a rotating local-history prompt.
